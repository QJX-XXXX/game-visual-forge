# Game music templates

These are original starting points for a brief. They are not model presets and do not override a user's instruments, duration, vocal policy, or loop requirement.

| Template | Function | Energy | Palette | Arrangement cue | Loop cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Menu air | menu / title | low | felt piano, marimba, soft pad | motif appears early, sparse second pass | return to opening voicing |
| Cozy puzzle | casual puzzle | low–medium | plucks, mallets, brushed percussion | small variations every 8–16 bars | steady ostinato, no hard stop |
| Arcade pulse | fast puzzle / arcade | medium–high | marimba, muted plucks, bass ostinato, tight drums | full groove on beat one; repeat a short motif without a breakdown | same rhythmic material at the seam, or a short instrumental cadence |
| Curious path | exploration | medium | nylon guitar, flute, hand percussion | add one colour at a time | open fifth or pad tail |
| Rising skirmish | regular combat | medium–high | muted strings, toms, synth pulse | clear build and short release | rhythm can re-enter on bar one |
| Boss pressure | boss encounter | high | low brass, taiko, distorted synth | repeated motif with controlled peak | use a dry downbeat at the seam |
| Tense room | stealth / danger | low–high | granular pad, piano harmonics, pulses | gradual density without a melody overload | unresolved but quiet seam |
| Bright result | win / results | medium | bells, pizzicato strings, warm brass | short fanfare then a calm tag | generally linear; loop only if requested |
| Solo study | independent pure music | low–medium | solo piano or chamber strings | complete arc with audible cadence | linear unless the user asks for a loop |

## Prompt rules

- Keep the template label out of the final prompt when it could be confused with a copyrighted work.
- Describe musical behaviour and instrumentation; do not ask for an artist imitation, cover, or voice clone.
- A normal BGM request uses `instrumental` unless the user explicitly requests vocals. `standalone-instrumental` always uses `instrumental`.
- The template is reference material only. The request object remains the source of truth.
